LAFLEURMUSIC is an iPod-style music player for your own music server — Navidrome and the other Subsonic-style servers. Same look as LAFLEURMUSIC on the computer, made for the phone. Keep albums on the phone and listen anywhere, even without network.

Listen

  • iPod-style menu: albums, artists, genres, songs, Top 100
  • Now Playing with Up Next, equalizer, crossfade and equal volume
  • Lock-screen, notification and Bluetooth controls
  • Swipe a song to play it next · long press for the menu

Your server

  • Navidrome and the Subsonic family (Airsonic, Gonic, LMS, Funkwhale, Nextcloud Music, Ampache)
  • Custom HTTP headers per account (Cloudflare Access, reverse proxies…)
  • Your password stays in the phone's keychain

Offline

  • Keep songs and albums on the phone: real files in a visible folder (Files → On My iPhone → LAFLEURMUSIC, or Music/LAFLEURMUSIC on Android)
  • Choose the folder — iCloud Drive included — and a space limit
  • Top 100 kept automatically at home on Wi-Fi
  • Outside, what isn't on the phone is greyed; listens are sent to the server when you're back
  • Music files you add to the folder yourself show up too

Install

  • Android 7+: download the APK and allow installing apps from this source when asked.
  • iPhone (iOS 15+): unsigned IPA.
