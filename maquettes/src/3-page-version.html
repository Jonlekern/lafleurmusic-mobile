Listen to the music on your Navidrome — or any Subsonic-style server — at home, with the same look as LAFLEURMUSIC on the computer.

What's in this version

  • iPod-style menu with all the albums on your server
  • Now Playing with Up Next, lock-screen and Bluetooth controls
  • Swipe a song to play it next, long press for the menu
  • Equal volume between songs (ReplayGain from the server)
  • Custom HTTP headers for each account

Good to know

  • Works on your home Wi-Fi for now.
  • Android: allow installing apps from this source when asked.
